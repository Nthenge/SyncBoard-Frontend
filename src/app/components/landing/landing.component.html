Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Overview

SyncBoard ("we", "us", "our") builds a collaborative workspace for teams. This policy explains what information we collect when you use SyncBoard, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We collect only what we need to run the product and keep it in sync — nothing more.

2. Information We Collect

Account information: name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text) when you register.

Workspace content: boards, lists, cards, comments, and attachments you or your teammates create.

Usage data: device type, browser, pages visited, and actions taken, collected automatically to help us fix bugs and improve performance.

Cookies: used to keep you signed in and remember basic preferences. We don't use third-party advertising cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate, maintain, and sync your boards in real time
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • To respond to support requests submitted through this site
  • To send essential service emails (password resets, security alerts)
  • To understand aggregate usage patterns and improve the product

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your workspace content to train any third-party models.

4. Data Sharing

We share data only with infrastructure providers who help us run SyncBoard — cloud hosting, database, and transactional email providers — each bound by a data processing agreement. We disclose information to authorities only when legally required to do so.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and workspace data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we permanently remove your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required for legal or security purposes.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the form on this page and select "Privacy Request" as the topic.

7. Security Measures

We encrypt data in transit and at rest and apply strict access controls internally. See our Security page for details.

8. Children's Privacy

SyncBoard is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we'll notify account holders by email before the changes take effect.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the contact form on this page, or email privacy@syncboard.app.

Trust

Security

Last updated: July 2026

Our Approach

Security isn't a checkbox for us — it's what makes real-time sync trustworthy in the first place. Here's what protects your data, described plainly rather than in vague marketing language.

Encryption

All traffic between your browser and SyncBoard is encrypted in transit via TLS 1.2+. Data at rest, including board content and attachments, is encrypted using industry-standard encryption at the database and storage layer.

Infrastructure & Hosting

SyncBoard runs on reputable cloud infrastructure with physical and network security managed by our hosting provider. We isolate environments between development, staging, and production.

Access Controls

Internal access to production systems and customer data is limited to engineers who need it, protected by multi-factor authentication, and logged. Passwords are never stored in plain text — we use salted, industry-standard hashing.

Application Security

We run automated dependency and vulnerability scanning on every release, and code changes go through peer review before deployment.

Incident Response

If we ever detect a security incident affecting your data, we'll notify affected account holders as soon as we can confirm the scope, along with what we're doing about it.

Responsible Disclosure

Found a security issue? We want to know. Email security@syncboard.app with details, and we'll respond within 2 business days. Please don't publicly disclose a vulnerability before we've had a chance to address it.

Compliance

SyncBoard is a growing product and we're honest about where we are: we follow security best practices day to day, and we're working toward formal certifications like SOC 2 as the company scales. We'll update this page as that progresses.

Questions

Reach the security team any time at security@syncboard.app.
